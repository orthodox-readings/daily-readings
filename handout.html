DailyReadings

The daily readings, feasts and fasts of the Orthodox Church — on your phone, in English,
following the Old Calendar of the Russian Church Abroad

Prepared for the parish of the Holy Dormition of the Mother of God, Corrimal
Russian Orthodox Church Abroad · Wollongong

Each day, in one place

The day's Epistle and Gospel, in traditional (KJV) or modern (NKJV) English — with every reading of the day, including Vespers and Matins.
The fasting rule for the day, stated plainly — what is asked, and what is allowed.
The day's commemorations and Lives of the Saints, with the full calendar one tap away.
Wisdom of the Fathers — a daily saying from the saints.
A month calendar of fasts and feasts, and the whole church year at a glance — every Great Feast and fasting season, for planning ahead.
The complete service texts in Church Slavonic (azbyka.ru), the day's troparia, and St Theophan's daily reflection — one tap each.
Works offline — the coming week is always saved. Free, no advertisements, no accounts, nothing to sign up for.

To install

Android: scan the code, then tap Install when the app offers, or Menu ⋮ → "Add to Home screen".

iPhone: scan the code in Safari, tap Share □↑ → "Add to Home Screen".

It then opens like any app, straight from your home screen.
