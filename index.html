    Fasting

    The colours show the Church’s fasting rule as set by the Typikon and kept in ROCOR, with the allowances for great feasts and ranked saints. It is the general norm: it does not apply in the same way to everyone, and a parish may keep a feast with greater solemnity. Personal fasting should be guided by your spiritual father, taking account of health and circumstances. Your notes stay on this device and are seen by no one else.

    About & sources

    Daily Readings is a companion for the daily readings, prayers, saints and fasts of the Orthodox Church as kept by the Russian Orthodox Church Outside of Russia (ROCOR), on the Old Calendar. It does not replace the guidance of your priest or spiritual father, nor the services of the Church.

    Fasting follows the Typikon as kept in ROCOR and is the Church’s general norm: it does not apply in the same way to everyone, and individual fasting should be discussed with your spiritual father. Liturgical colours follow the ROCOR Liturgical Handbook (Diocese of Great Britain and Western Europe); a parish may differ.

    Readings & saints via orthocal.info — its Slavic tradition is maintained against the OCA’s calendar. Where ROCOR differs and the difference has been confirmed, the app corrects it and says so on the day. English Scripture: King James Version.

    Lives of the Saints: Abbamoses.com (John Brady), used with permission via orthocal. The Lives follow the Greek Horologion, whose date for a saint occasionally differs from the Slavic calendar by a day or two.

    New King James Version® © 1982 Thomas Nelson.

    Greek New Testament: Byzantine Textform, ed. Robinson & Pierpont (2018) — public domain. Patriarchal Text of 1904/1912 (Antoniades), transcribed by M. A. Robinson & J. Ala-Konni — public domain.

    Sayings of the Fathers are a parish selection. Each shows its source where it has been confirmed; adapted wording is marked.
