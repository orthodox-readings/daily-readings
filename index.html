    About & sources

    Readings & calendar via orthocal.info — Slavic Old-Calendar tradition. English text of the daily readings: King James Version.

    Lives of the Saints: Abbamoses.com (John Brady), used with permission via orthocal. The Lives follow the Greek Horologion, whose date for a saint occasionally differs from the Slavic calendar by a day or two.

    New King James Version® © 1982 Thomas Nelson (unofficial text; KJV shown if unavailable). Shown for the daily readings only.

    Scripture reader: King James Version (public domain; text from eBible.org).

    Greek New Testament: Byzantine Textform, ed. Robinson & Pierpont (2018) — public domain. Patriarchal Text of 1904/1912 (Antoniades), transcribed by M. A. Robinson & J. Ala-Konni — public domain.

    Sayings of the Fathers are a parish selection; some are adapted or paraphrased and are marked so. Liturgical colours follow common Russian practice and are a guide only.
