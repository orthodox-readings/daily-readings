About & sources

Daily Readings is a companion for the daily readings, prayers, saints and fasts of the Orthodox Church on the Old Calendar. It does not replace the guidance of your priest or spiritual father, nor the services of the Church.

Readings & calendar via orthocal.info — Slavic Old-Calendar tradition. English Scripture: King James Version.

Lives of the Saints: Abbamoses.com (John Brady), used with permission via orthocal. The Lives follow the Greek Horologion, whose date for a saint occasionally differs from the Slavic calendar by a day or two.

New King James Version® © 1982 Thomas Nelson.

Greek New Testament: Byzantine Textform, ed. Robinson & Pierpont (2018) — public domain. Patriarchal Text of 1904/1912 (Antoniades), transcribed by M. A. Robinson & J. Ala-Konni — public domain.

Sayings of the Fathers are a parish selection. Each shows its source where it has been confirmed; adapted wording is marked. Liturgical colours follow common Russian practice and are a guide only.
